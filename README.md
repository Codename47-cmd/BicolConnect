# BicolConnect

### A Community-Based Business, Services, and Opportunities Platform

**BicolConnect** is a proposed web-based platform designed to connect local community members with **businesses, service providers, and opportunities** in Sorsogon and the wider Bicol community.

Unlike a simple business directory, BicolConnect focuses on creating an interactive connection between residents and local businesses through **business discovery, service requests, and opportunity applications**.

> **Discover. Request. Apply. Connect.**

## 🌐 Live Demo

**Try the interactive prototype:**

👉 https://bicolconnect.netlify.app/

The live website demonstrates the proposed user interface, navigation, business discovery experience, and core platform concept.

---

## 🎯 Project Purpose

Many community members may have difficulty finding local businesses, available services, and opportunities in one convenient place. At the same time, small local businesses and service providers may have limited digital visibility.

BicolConnect proposes a centralized platform where users can discover local businesses and services, request assistance, and explore opportunities offered by businesses within their community.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🔎 Business & Service Discovery

Browse local businesses and service providers through an organized and easy-to-use interface.

### 📩 Ask for a Service

Users can provide feedback about businesses and services.

### 📍 Location & Map

Users can explore businesses based on their location and discover nearby services.

The proposed system includes concepts for reporting inappropriate information and verifying business listings.

## 🧩 Core Concept

BicolConnect is built around three main functions:

### FIND → ASK → APPLY

🔎 **FIND**
Discover local businesses and services.

📩 **ASK**
Request a service from a local provider.

💼 **APPLY**
Discover and apply for available opportunities.

These functions transform the project from a basic business directory into a more interactive **community connection platform**.

---

## 🖥️ Project Type

BicolConnect is currently an **interactive front-end prototype** developed for academic presentation and system design purposes.

The live prototype demonstrates the proposed interface, navigation, user experience, and major system concepts.

### Current Limitations

* No production backend
* No real database
* No real authentication system
* Uses sample/demo information
* Service requests are simulated
* Applications are simulated
* Some functionality is designed for demonstration purposes

A future implementation could introduce a backend, database, user accounts, real-time information, notifications, and additional security features.
